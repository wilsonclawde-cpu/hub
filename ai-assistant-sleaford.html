What it is

An AI assistant set up around your specific business: your services, prices, opening hours, tone of voice, the questions customers always ask and the way you like things done. It can draft email and message replies for you to approve, answer enquiries on your website or Facebook page, summarise what came in overnight, keep a to-do list moving and take the first pass at quotes, bookings and paperwork. It doesn't replace you; it gives you a capable assistant who never goes off sick.

What you get

  • An assistant configured with your business knowledge — services, prices, hours, policies, FAQs, house style
  • Connected to where the work happens: email, website chat, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, your calendar and forms as appropriate
  • Clear rules about what it does on its own and what it drafts for you to check — you stay in control
  • A morning summary of what needs your attention, if you want one
  • Training for you and your team — half an hour is usually enough
  • Optional monthly support to keep it accurate as prices, hours and services change

AI assistant for small business Sleaford — FAQs

How much does an AI assistant cost for a small business?

Set-up starts from £199, fixed quote, covering configuration, connection to your channels and training. Optional monthly support keeps it up to date. Any underlying software subscriptions are yours and are explained up front.

Will it say something wrong to a customer?

We set it up to draft rather than send on anything it isn't sure about, and it's told to say "I'll check with the team" rather than guess. You choose how much autonomy it gets, and you can dial it back at any time.

Is my customer data safe?

The assistant only sees what you give it access to, under your accounts. We don't keep copies of your data and we'll explain plainly where information goes before anything is connected.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can send an email, you can work with it. Training takes about half an hour.

What's the difference between this and admin automation?

Automation handles fixed, repeatable steps — send this when that happens. The assistant handles language and judgement — reading a message, understanding it, drafting a sensible reply. They work well together.
