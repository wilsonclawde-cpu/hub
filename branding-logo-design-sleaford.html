What it is

Your brand is what people see before they've spoken to you: the logo on the van, the sign over the door, the header on the quote. If it looks like it was made in Word in 2009, that's what they assume about the rest of the business. We design a simple, confident identity that does the opposite — and we give you everything you need to use it consistently.

What you get

  • A logo designed for your business, presented as two or three directions to choose from, then refined with you
  • Variations: full colour, single colour, reversed for dark backgrounds, icon-only for social profiles
  • A colour palette and font pairing that work together on screen and in print
  • Every file format you'll be asked for — SVG, PNG, PDF, plus a version sized for social
  • A one-page brand sheet showing how to use it all, so a sign-writer or printer gets it right first time
  • Optional extras: business cards, van livery layout, shop signage artwork, email signature, social templates

Who it's for

New businesses in Sleaford that need to start off looking credible. Established ones whose logo has been "temporary" for eight years. Anyone whose brand doesn't survive being shrunk to a phone screen or blown up on a van.

How it works

We start with a short conversation about what you do, who you sell to and what you want people to feel. We come back with initial directions; you pick one; we refine it until it's right. You get the full set of files and the brand sheet, and you own the lot — no licence fees, no strings. From brief to finished files is usually a week or two.

Why local matters

We've branded our own businesses on Kesteven Street and built their websites to match, so we know what works on a Sleaford high street as well as on a screen. We'll happily talk you through it over a coffee downstairs. If you want the new look carried across your social channels, we do that too.

Serving Sleaford and the surrounding area

We design brands for businesses in Sleaford and across Lincolnshire — Grantham, Lincoln, Newark-on-Trent, Boston, Bourne, Spalding and Stamford — and for village businesses in Ruskington, Heckington, Billinghay, Metheringham, Navenby and Ancaster. Most of the work happens by email; the first conversation can happen anywhere in North Kesteven.

Logo design Sleaford — FAQs

How much does a logo cost in Sleaford?

Logo and branding starts from £149, which covers the logo, its variations, colours, fonts, every file format and a one-page brand sheet. Extras like business cards or signage artwork are quoted separately and fixed before we start.

How many revisions do I get?

Enough to get it right. We present a few directions, you choose one, and we refine it with you. We'd rather spend an extra round than hand over something you're lukewarm about.

Do I own the logo?

Yes, fully. No licences, no ongoing fees, and you get the editable source files.

Can you refresh my existing logo rather than start again?

Yes. Often the right answer is a careful tidy-up that keeps what customers recognise. We'll tell you which we think you need.

Can you handle printing and signage?

We prepare print-ready artwork to the printer's or sign-maker's spec and liaise with them if you'd like. We can recommend local suppliers.
