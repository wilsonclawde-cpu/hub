What it is

A straight-talking look at the businesses you're really competing with in Sleaford and the surrounding towns — not a 60-page deck, but a report you'll read in one sitting and act on the same week. We look at pricing, offers, websites, Google rankings, reviews, social activity and the small things customers notice, and we tell you where the gaps are.

What you get

  • Up to five local competitors chosen with you — the ones you actually lose work to
  • Side-by-side comparison: prices and packages, opening hours, services, guarantees, what they say about themselves
  • Online footprint: how they rank for the searches that matter, Google Business quality, review count and sentiment, website speed and mobile experience
  • Where they're winning, where they're weak, and where nobody in the area is doing a good job
  • A shortlist of quick wins, ranked by effort and payoff, plus the longer-term moves worth planning
  • A half-hour walkthrough so nothing in the report is a mystery

Who it's for

Owners who've noticed the phone go quieter and want to know why. Businesses about to change their prices, open a second site, or spend money on marketing and would rather know the lie of the land first. New businesses in Sleaford deciding how to position themselves.

How it works

Tell us who you're up against (or ask us to work it out). We spend a few days digging — public pricing, websites, listings, reviews, social channels, and where it's appropriate, a phone call or visit as a customer would make. You get the written report and a walkthrough. From there, you can act on it yourself or hand us the bits you'd like done — local SEO, a sharper website or a brand refresh.

Why local matters

National benchmarks tell you nothing about Sleaford. We know the local market because we trade in it, and we know how to compare a business on Southgate with one on the Grantham road without pretending they're the same. We'll also tell you, honestly, if a competitor is simply better on something — that's useful to know too.

Serving Sleaford and the surrounding area

We analyse competitors for businesses in Sleaford and across the region — Grantham, Lincoln, Newark-on-Trent, Boston, Bourne, Spalding and Stamford — as well as village businesses in Ruskington, Heckington, Billinghay, Metheringham, Navenby and Ancaster, where your real competition might be one firm in the next village and one in town. Competitor analysis Sleaford — FAQs

How much does a competitor analysis cost?

From £99 for a written report on up to five local competitors, with a walkthrough call. Larger markets or more competitors are quoted individually before we start.

How do you get the information?

Public sources only: websites, Google, review platforms, social channels, directories and published prices. Where it's fair and normal, we'll make an enquiry the way a customer would. We don't do anything underhand.

Is it just online stuff?

No. We look at pricing, offers, service and presentation as well as digital presence. Plenty of gaps in Sleaford have nothing to do with the internet.

How quickly do I get the report?

Typically within a week of agreeing the competitor list.

What if I want you to act on it?

That's up to you. The report is yours to use however you like. If you'd like help with the fixes, our other services — local SEO, web design, branding, social — pick up where the report leaves off.
