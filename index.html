Questions people ask us

Where is TAG Sleaford?

Kesteven Street, Sleaford, Lincolnshire NG34 7DT — the old police station, opposite the council offices and a short walk from the town centre and the railway station. The coffee shop is on the ground floor, the offices are above, and there's parking directly behind.

Do you only work with businesses in Sleaford?

Sleaford is home, but no. We work with businesses across Lincolnshire — Grantham, Lincoln, Newark-on-Trent, Boston, Bourne, Spalding, Stamford and the villages of North Kesteven such as Ruskington, Heckington, Billinghay, Metheringham, Navenby and Ancaster. Most of the work happens by phone and email anyway; if you'd rather sit down, the coffee shop is downstairs.

How do I get in touch about business services?

Use the enquiry form at centre.tagsleaford.com and choose "Business services", or call 01522 424963. Tell us what you're after and we'll come back with a fixed price.

Are the prices fixed?

Each service has a "from" price, and we give you a fixed quote up front before any work starts. The price you're told is the price you pay.

Can I rent an office and get help with my website?

Yes. Offices at Kesteven Business Centre start from £250 a month all-inclusive, and the same enquiry form covers both — tell us what you need and we'll sort it in one conversation.

What are the coffee shop's opening hours?

Monday to Friday 7:30am–4:30pm and Sunday 8am–12pm. The Kesteven Lounge opens on Thursday and Friday evenings. Call 01529 707123 for orders and catering.
