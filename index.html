FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Where is TAG Sleaford?

Kesteven Street, Sleaford, Lincolnshire NG34 7DT — the old police station, opposite the council offices and a short walk from the town centre and the railway station. GLOW Sleaford, the superfood café, is on the ground floor, the offices are above, and there's parking directly behind.

Do you only work with businesses in Sleaford?

Sleaford is home, but no. We work with businesses across Lincolnshire — Grantham, Lincoln, Newark-on-Trent, Boston, Bourne, Spalding, Stamford and the villages of North Kesteven such as Ruskington, Heckington, Billinghay, Metheringham, Navenby and Ancaster. Most of the work happens by phone and email anyway; if you'd rather sit down, GLOW Sleaford café is downstairs.

How do I get in touch about business services?

Use the enquiry form at centre.tagsleaford.com and choose "Business services", or call 01522 424963. Tell us what you're after and we'll come back with a fixed price.

Are the prices fixed?

Each service has a "from" price, and we give you a fixed quote up front before any work starts. The price you're told is the price you pay.

Can I rent an office and get help with my website?

Yes. Offices at Kesteven Business Centre start from £250 a month all-inclusive, and the same enquiry form covers both — tell us what you need and we'll sort it in one conversation.

What are GLOW Sleaford's opening hours?

GLOW Sleaford is open Monday to Friday, 8am–4.30pm, and closed on Saturday and Sunday (see glowsleaford.com for the latest). Walk in, order for delivery across Sleaford, or find GLOW on Uber Eats. Call 01529 707123 for orders and catering.
