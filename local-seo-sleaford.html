What it is

Local SEO is the work that puts your business in front of people searching nearby — the Google map pack, the "near me" results and the listings that show your opening hours, phone number and reviews before anyone reaches your website. It is mostly unglamorous, methodical work, which is exactly why so few small businesses ever do it properly.

What you get

  • Google Business Profile claimed, verified, completed and optimised — categories, services, description, photos, hours, service area
  • Your website tuned for local searches: Sleaford and Lincolnshire in the right places, structured data, location pages where they earn their place
  • Consistent name, address and phone across the directories that matter (Bing, Apple Maps, Yell and the rest)
  • A simple system for asking happy customers for reviews — and replying to them
  • A plain-English report showing what changed and what to expect next
  • Optional monthly upkeep: posts, photo updates, Q&A, review replies and ranking checks

Who it's for

Any business whose customers come from a radius rather than a nation: trades, restaurants, salons, dentists, garages, estate agents, gyms, solicitors. If a customer would search for what you do with a town name attached, local SEO is where your marketing money works hardest.

How it works

We audit what Google currently shows for your business and your main competitors in Sleaford. Then we fix and complete your Google Business Profile, tidy up your website's local signals, and sort out any listings that are wrong or missing. You get a short report of what was done. If you want it kept sharp, we can take it on monthly — no long contract.

Why local matters

We know which searches Sleaford people actually make, because we run two businesses here that depend on them. Combine this with a fast website and a look at what your rivals are doing in our competitor analysis, and you have the three things that decide who gets found.

Local SEO in Sleaford — FAQs

What does local SEO cost in Sleaford?

Local SEO and Google Business set-up starts from £149 as a one-off. If you'd like it looked after monthly — posts, reviews, updates and ranking checks — we'll quote a fixed monthly fee with no long contract.

How long before I see results?

Google Business changes can show within days; ranking improvements in the map pack usually build over a few weeks as Google re-crawls and reviews come in. We'll be honest about what's realistic for your category and area.

Do I need a website for this to work?

A Google Business Profile works on its own, but a fast website that mentions Sleaford in the right places makes it work far better. We can do either or both.

Is this the same as Google Ads?

No. Local SEO is about earning your place in the free results and the map pack. Ads are paid and stop the moment you stop paying. We focus on the part that keeps working.

Can you help with bad or missing reviews?

We can set up a simple way to ask customers for reviews, draft replies to the ones you have, and advise on the ones you'd rather not have. We never post fake reviews.
