What it is

A steady stream of posts, stories and replies on the channels your customers actually use — for most Sleaford businesses that's Facebook first, then Instagram, sometimes Google Business posts and LinkedIn. Written in your voice, planned a month ahead, and posted for you. The aim isn't going viral; it's being the business people think of first because they saw you last week.

What you get

  • A monthly content plan agreed with you in ten minutes — offers, seasonal hooks, what's new, what's local
  • Posts written and designed to your brand, scheduled and published
  • Captions that sound like a person, not a marketing department
  • Replies to comments and messages during working hours, or a heads-up when something needs you
  • Photos and short videos edited from what you send us — or shot for you on site in Sleaford
  • A short monthly note on what got seen, what got clicks and what to do more of

Who it's for

Cafés, shops, salons, trades, fitness, hospitality, professional services — any business whose customers are local and scroll in the evening. Especially owners who know they should post more and haven't since March.

How it works

We set up or tidy your profiles, agree the tone and the plan, and get going. Each month you send us the raw material — a photo of the new menu, a job you're proud of, a date to remember — and we turn it into a month of posts. Everything is scheduled in advance so it goes out even when you're flat out. Month to month, no long contract.

Why local matters

We post for our own café (GLOW Sleaford) and business centre, so we know what Sleaford people engage with and what they scroll past. Local content works best when it's actually local — the market, the show, the roadworks, the school holidays — and you can't fake that from a city agency. It also pairs naturally with a brand refresh and Google Business posts.

Social media management Sleaford — FAQs

What does social media management cost?

From £99 a month for planned, written and scheduled content on your main channel. More channels, more posts, replies and photography are quoted as a fixed monthly fee. Month to month, no long tie-in.

Which platforms do you cover?

Facebook and Instagram for most local businesses; Google Business posts, LinkedIn and TikTok where they make sense for you. We'll recommend where to spend the effort rather than spreading it thin.

Will it sound like me?

Yes. We spend time getting your tone right at the start, and you approve the plan each month. If a post doesn't sound like you, it doesn't go out.

Do I need to supply photos?

It helps — a quick phone photo of real work beats stock every time. We tidy and edit what you send, and can visit to shoot in Sleaford if needed.

Do you run paid ads too?

We can set up and run modest local boosts and ad campaigns where they'll pay back. We'll tell you when they won't.
