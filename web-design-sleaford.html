What it is

A proper website for a real business: one that tells people what you do, where you are, what it costs and how to get hold of you — and does it fast, on any screen. Not a template with your logo dropped in, and not a six-month agency project with a six-figure feel. We build clean, quick, mobile-first sites and we host them, so once it's live there's nothing for you to manage.

What you get

  • A modern design built around your business, not a theme somebody else is also using
  • The words written for you — plain English, in your voice, aimed at the customers you actually want
  • Mobile-first build that loads quickly on a patchy signal outside Sleaford as well as on fibre in town
  • Local SEO basics baked in: Sleaford-focused titles, descriptions, structured data and a Google Business link-up
  • Contact forms that land in your inbox, click-to-call numbers, opening hours and a map
  • Hosting, SSL certificate and small tweaks sorted for a few pounds a month
  • A fixed quote before we start, and a site you own outright

Who it's for

Trades, shops, salons, cafés, consultants, clinics, clubs — any Sleaford or Lincolnshire business that needs to be found and trusted online. If your current site is embarrassing on a phone, was built by someone who has since vanished, or simply doesn't exist yet, this is the fix.

How it works

We start with a short conversation (in person at GLOW, the café downstairs, or on the phone) about what you do and who you want more of. You send us whatever you have — photos, a price list, an old brochure — and we draft the site. You review it, we tweak it, we put it live on your domain. Most straightforward sites are live within days of sign-off. Ongoing hosting and small changes are covered by a low monthly fee.

Why local matters

We run Kesteven Business Centre and GLOW Sleaford from the same building on Kesteven Street, and the sites you're looking at are ours — built the way we'd build yours. When you need something changed, you're talking to someone down the road, not a ticket queue. Pair the site with local SEO and Google Business and it starts earning its keep.

Web design in Sleaford — FAQs

How much does a website cost in Sleaford?

Websites start from £349 for a professional, mobile-friendly site — designed, written, built and hosted. We give a fixed quote up front, so the price you're told is the price you pay.

How long does it take?

Most straightforward business sites are live within days of sign-off, not months. Larger sites with lots of pages or an online shop take longer, and we'll tell you how long before you commit.

Do I need to sort hosting or a domain myself?

No. We handle hosting, SSL and the domain set-up. If you already own a domain, we'll point it at the new site for you.

Will people be able to find the site on Google?

Every site we build includes the local SEO basics — Sleaford-focused titles and descriptions, structured data and a link to your Google Business Profile. For ongoing ranking work, see our Local SEO service from £149.

Do you work with businesses outside Sleaford?

Yes. We work with businesses across Lincolnshire — Grantham, Lincoln, Newark, Boston, Bourne, Spalding, Stamford and the villages in between.
